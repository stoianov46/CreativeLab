# Pricing Strategy

Status: **IN PROGRESS — every category has now been searched once, and the evidence is thin everywhere.** Almost all baht figures come from Bangkok agencies' own price pages; no source is independent, and none covers Koh Samui or Koh Phangan.
Figures live in [`src/content/pricing.ts`](src/content/pricing.ts) ([PRICING_MASTER.md](PRICING_MASTER.md)). All prices are indicative.

## Method

1. Find publicly listed 2026 prices for comparable services in Thailand (Koh Phangan, Koh Samui, Phuket, Bangkok), with credible global benchmarks only as a secondary reference.
2. Record every source, the date seen and what it does and does not say.
3. Position CreativeLAB in the upper-middle / premium mid-market. The owner's brief suggests roughly 10–20% above comparable market averages **only where evidence and scope justify it**. No automatic markup; no copying competitor prices.
4. Ad spend is never part of an agency fee and is always shown separately.

- The current Advertising ranges (`google-ads.management` THB 12,000–30,000, `meta-ads.management` 10,000–25,000/month) sit inside the Bangkok evidence. ## How the current ranges compare

- **Consistent with the evidence:** local SEO retainer (ours ฿10,000–25,000/month vs ฿10,000–50,000), Google Business Profile setup (ours ฿5,000–10,000 vs "from ฿3,500"), brand identity (ours ฿30,000–80,000 vs ฿20,000–100,000+), villa photography (ours ฿6,000–12,000 half-day vs ฿5,000–12,000), full food-photography shoots, and the advertising retainers.
- **Possibly low:** the drone add-on to a photo shoot (ours ฿3,000–6,000 vs ฿5,000–10,000 — the standalone drone video row at ฿5,000–12,000 is in line).
- **Not testable:** video production, social media management, graphic design and the e-commerce and web-design ranges above the entry level — the evidence is a handful of USD or agency-asking figures.
- **No evidence supports a 10–20% premium** over a market average, and no average could be computed from this material. **No price was changed.** The drone add-on is flagged for the owner.
